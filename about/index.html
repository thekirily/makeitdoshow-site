Films about everyday life in America, from the 1930s to the 1970s, told with real film and the records people kept.

The kitchen table. The price of a loaf. The story that starts when someone says, “I remember that.” Make It Do follows those small things back to their sources, and makes keepsakes that give families a place to add their own memories.
