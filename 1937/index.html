The record behind the memory

Where the numbers come from

Food and gas comparisons use the supplied 2025 BLS averages. Postage is 82¢ in November 2026. These are dated reference prices, not a live price feed. Milk then was home-delivered; the reference is store-bought. Fuel, products, and coverage have changed.

  • Historical Statistics of the United States, Part 1, p. 213, series E 187–202
  • EIA, Annual Energy Review, table 5.24: regular gasoline, nominal prices
  • USPS, Rates for Domestic Letters Since 1863
  • Department of Labor, History of Federal Minimum Wage Rates
  • BLS Average Price Data: 2025 monthly observations
  • USPS, Forever stamps: 82¢, checked November 5, 2026
  • BLS Consumer Price Index, series CUUR0000SA0
