Privacy policy

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you ask for a free download or buy something, so we can send it and, if you agree, write to you about new films and products. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe.

  • Basic visit counts (which page and which film a visit came from), without selling or sharing them for advertising.

Who else handles it

Only the services needed to deliver what you asked for: Gumroad (checkout and delivery), our print and shipping partner (your delivery address, for printed orders), and our email provider. We do not sell your information.

YouTube API Services

Our internal tool «Channels Agent» uses YouTube API Services to publish and measure videos on our own YouTube channels only. It does not access any viewer's account. YouTube's terms: youtube.com/t/terms. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what we hold about you, or to stop all emails, write to chaddoto2@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days. We keep order records as long as tax law requires, and everything else only as long as it is useful to you.
