Privacy, in plain English.

Make It Do, The Bible As It Was and The Large Print. Updated November 5, 2026.

Your free sheet

You can read the prices and download every free sheet and the recipe cards without giving us an email address. A direct download does not sign you up for email.

Visit and film counts

When configured, Make It Do uses PostHog to count page visits, year selections, downloads, sample opens, and checkout clicks. We send the page path, film number, entry door (spoken address, QR, or description), a random visit ID, and a referrer hostname or broad source category. We do not send email addresses, names, shipping addresses, payment details, or full referring URLs to analytics. Analytics providers may receive the IP address used to connect to them.

Optional visit analytics start only after you choose Allow analytics. Until then, no analytics identifier or attribution is stored and no analytics request is sent. With permission, sessionStorage keeps the entry door, campaign tags, random ID, and any experiment assignment during a visit. LocalStorage remembers your consent choice. You can withdraw permission here; this removes stored visit attribution. DNT and GPC override permission. Optional experiments follow the same choice. No advertising cookies, session recordings, heatmaps, fingerprinting, or automatic form capture are installed. We honor Global Privacy Control and Do Not Track. Counts are incomplete when tracking is blocked or declined.

Email updates

Optional email signup, when enabled, uses Brevo to send a double confirmation link. Only confirming that link subscribes you to updates. Your email is sent directly to Brevo, never to visit analytics. Unsubscribe in any update. Downloading a free file is separate from marketing permission.

Email and checkout

When email delivery or ordering is enabled, you leave this site for Gumroad. Gumroad collects the email and any details needed to deliver the selected product. Choosing a free file here does not imply agreement to marketing. Marketing choices are presented at checkout; use the unsubscribe link in any marketing message or email us to stop it.

For printed products, the printer and shipping provider need the recipient’s name, delivery address, and product details. Our planned print setup uses Prodigi for booklets and standard hardcovers, with Lulu for the cloth Heirloom edition. Printed orders are not open yet. We do not see your card number. Gumroad handles payment, receipts, applicable taxes, and its refund process.

When the sales integration is configured, verified paid orders and completed free Gumroad signups with analytics permission carried from this site are counted in PostHog with film, door, product, year, currency, amount, and a pseudonymous event identifier. Customer emails and postal addresses are not sent. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising.

Other services

GitHub Pages hosts this site and may keep technical request logs. The page links to YouTube and public archives; their own policies apply when you visit. PostHog privacy, Gumroad privacy, GitHub privacy.

YouTube API Services

Our internal tool “Channels Agent” uses YouTube API Services to publish and measure videos on our own channels only. It does not access a viewer’s account. YouTube terms; Google privacy. Access granted to the tool can be revoked at Google account permissions.

Your choices and retention

To see, correct, or delete information we hold, or to stop emails, write to chaddoto2@gmail.com. We aim to answer within 30 days. Order records are retained as required for accounting; delivery details are used to fulfill and support orders. Browser attribution is limited to the current tab session. Analytics retention follows the configured project’s retention setting; request deletion by contacting us.
