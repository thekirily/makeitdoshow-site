A few practical questions

Can I give this to Mom or Dad?

Yes. Use their first name for the cover and their birth year. When ordering opens, you can have the printed copy sent to their U.S. address. The buyer receives the PDF.

Are all the pictures from the chosen year?

No. The prices and names are specific to the year. Color film frames show everyday life in the period and carry the actual film’s title and date. They are not presented as family photographs.

Does my parent need to use an app?

The printed booklet can be enjoyed without a phone, account, or app. The digital PDF opens in a standard PDF reader.

What if the book arrives damaged or a detail is wrong?

Write to chaddoto2@gmail.com. We will work with you to correct the problem. Read our order terms.
